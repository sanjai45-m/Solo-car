# 3D Racing Car Generation Prompts

These prompts are designed for creating **3D racing cars** that can be used as vehicle assets in a **2D arcade racing game** and displayed in a **3D car selection/garage screen**.

## 1. Red Hypercar — “Inferno X”

```text
Create a high-performance futuristic 3D racing car called “Inferno X”, designed for a fast arcade racing game. Low and wide aerodynamic body, aggressive front splitter, sharp LED headlights, large air intakes, sculpted side panels, wide racing tires, large rear diffuser, dramatic rear wing, mid-engine supercar proportions. Glossy deep red body with subtle black carbon-fiber sections and metallic details. Extremely sporty and premium appearance. Clean hard-surface 3D modeling, realistic PBR materials, detailed rims and tires, game-ready topology, no driver, no background objects. Show the complete car from a front three-quarter angle, centered, studio lighting, isolated on a transparent or plain background, suitable for a 3D car selection screen in a 2D racing game.
```

## Recommended Generation Settings

Use consistent settings for all five cars:

- **Camera:** Front three-quarter view
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1
- **Background:** Transparent if supported
- **Style:** Realistic 3D game asset
- **Materials:** PBR
- **Geometry:** Clean hard-surface / game-ready
- **Lighting:** Studio lighting
- **Vehicle:** Full car visible
- **Wheels:** All wheels visible
- **No driver**
- **No logos**
- **No text**
- **No environment**
- **Consistent proportions across all cars**

## Game Asset Goal

The five cars should feel like they belong to the **same racing game**, while having clearly different silhouettes and identities. They should work both as:

1. **3D rotating models** in the garage/car-selection screen.
2. **2D racing-game vehicles** during gameplay.
3. **Upgrade/customization assets** for future features such as speed, acceleration, handling, braking, and nitro.
